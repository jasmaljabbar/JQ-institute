Is JG Institute suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Every program starts from the fundamentals. Junior programs need no prior knowledge, and AI programs assume only basic computer use.

What age groups do you teach?

Junior Discovery Programs are for children aged 4 to 14, grouped as 4–6, 7–10 and 11–14 years. AI Web Development, Digital Marketing and Python are open to learners aged 15+ (10th standard or above). AI HR & People Analytics is for graduates.

Can I attend a free demo class before joining?

Yes. Use any "Book Free Demo" button to choose a program and time — we'll call to confirm your slot.

Do you offer weekend or online batches?

Weekend batches run for all Junior programs, and selected AI programs offer live online sessions. Ask our team for the current batch schedule.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes. Learners who complete their modules, assignments, assessments and projects receive a JG Institute certificate.

Do you help with projects, internships and placement?

AI learners build a project portfolio, receive internship information and get placement support — resume review, interview preparation and career guidance.

Are instalment payments or scholarships available?

Flexible payment options, early-bird discounts and scholarships are available for eligible learners. Our team will share the details during counselling.

Where is JG Institute located?

We're in Ambalavayal, Wayanad, Kerala. See the map and directions.
