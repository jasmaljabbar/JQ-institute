FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

What is the age criteria for Junior Programs?

Junior Discovery Programs are for children aged 4 to 14, in three age groups: 4–6, 7–10 and 11–14 years.

Who can join the AI Integrated Programs?

Web Development, Digital Marketing and Python Development are open to learners aged 15+ who have passed 10th standard or above. AI Human Resources & People Analytics is open to any graduate.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes. Learners who complete their modules, assignments, assessments and projects receive a JG Institute certificate.

Are the classes online or offline?

Please contact us for the current class format and batch options.

Can I switch my program after joining?

Please speak to our counsellors about switching programs.

Is there any scholarship available?

Merit-based scholarships and early-bird discounts are offered. Contact us for current eligibility and dates.

How can I pay the fees?

Flexible payment options are available. Our team will explain the payment methods during enrolment.
